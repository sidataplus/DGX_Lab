# Trademarks and public-release boundary

DGX Lab is an independent educational simulator. It must not use NVIDIA logos, imitate NVIDIA visual identity, imply endorsement, or present itself as an official NVIDIA or Slurm product.

The name **DGX Lab** is retained as the approved internal product name. Before public distribution, complete a focused trademark review and retain the following disclaimer in product metadata and documentation:

> DGX Lab is an independent educational simulator and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by NVIDIA Corporation or SchedMD LLC.

Use generic original illustrations. Hardware and command names may be used descriptively where needed to teach compatible concepts.
